Songkhla is not one story — it is many, layered across centuries and told through the food of its people. Choose your journey: from a single-day immersion into the city's three eras, to a three-day rail odyssey through the flavours of the south. Every route leads somewhere unforgettable.

Three Ages of Songkhla

Shape history with your own hands. On the legendary Sangkhalok Road—Sukhothai’s heritage craft lane—you’ll learn to hand-form a traditional Sangkhalok plate and paint it with symbolic patterns like fish, rice fields, or gentle water flows.

Gastronomic Echo

A journey where every taste tells a story. Over three days and two nights, local trains, historic towns, and hidden villages become part of your sensory voyage through the culinary landscapes of southern Thailand. Each province offers a different flavour — Songkhla's multicultural depth, Phatthalung's gentle wilderness, Bang Klam's golden sweetness, and Hatyai's vibrant streets.

Book This Experience

Sweet Buzz of Bang Klam

Ride the local train to Bang Klam — a fertile agricultural gem of Songkhla with roots stretching to the Ayutthaya period. Discover the world of stingless bees, taste fresh honey from the hive, and enjoy a warm picnic lunch of grandmothers' recipes from Tassapan. A gentle, heartwarming day filled with smiles and sweet memories.

Book This Experience

Phatthalung Rhythm

Phatthalung is like a backyard herbal garden — quietly holding treasures of wisdom through local food traditions, cultural arts, and timeless folklore. The gentle rhythm of the train leads you into the heart of it all: shadow puppet painting, pounding chili dips, stirring sago pudding, sipping coffee in taro fields, and browsing markets where every souvenir is woven with local stories.
